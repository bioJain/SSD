# Research-question tree

## Root decision

**RQ0. Should the project recommend advance, defer or stop for continued
portfolio diligence on a B-cell reset opportunity in Sjögren disease, and under
what patient, modality and evidence conditions?**

- **Owner:** JHA-141
- **Final output:** Integrated decision map, gate assessment and recommendation
- **Decision gates:** G1–G6 in the project charter

Every child question below must resolve part of RQ0. A finding that does not map to
a question and output is contextual, not decision-critical.

## RQ1 — What counts as B-cell reset?

- **Owner:** [JHA-142](https://linear.app/jha-res/issue/JHA-142/define-b-cell-reset-taxonomy)
- **Output:** Operational taxonomy, four-axis framework and claim-boundary rules
- **Feeds:** Modality profiles, competitive comparison, scoring rubric and final
  patient–modality decision map
- **Gates:** G2, G4, G5

### RQ1.1 Classification

- How will transient depletion, deep depletion, immune reset, functional control
  and unproven reset claims be operationally distinguished?
- What minimum evidence is required for each category?
- When must a company reset claim be relabeled as interpretation or unknown?

### RQ1.2 Four-axis performance

- What evidence establishes depletion depth?
- What evidence establishes tissue and compartment reach?
- What constitutes off-treatment durability rather than continued pharmacologic
  control?
- What indicates the quality of immune reconstitution or tolerance restoration?

### RQ1.3 Boundaries and edge cases

- How are plasma-cell effects represented?
- How are incomplete tissue data and missing reconstitution data classified?
- Which examples test the taxonomy without allowing marketing language to decide
  classification?

## RQ2 — Can every decision claim be audited?

- **Owner:** [JHA-143](https://linear.app/jha-res/issue/JHA-143/build-evidence-ledger-and-claim-taxonomy)
- **Output:** Evidence Ledger schema, data dictionary and controlled vocabulary
- **Feeds:** Every research memo, model, table, score and final recommendation
- **Gate:** G3; control layer for all other gates

### RQ2.1 Traceability

- What fields link each material claim and number to a source URL, publication
  date, access date and precise evidence location?
- How will direct versus indirect evidence and Sjögren-direct versus extrapolated
  evidence be labeled?
- How will a final statement link back to one or more ledger rows?

### RQ2.2 Interpretation and confidence

- Is each entry a fact, company interpretation, analyst inference,
  extrapolation or unknown?
- What confidence scale is used, and what lowers confidence?
- How are contradictory sources preserved rather than averaged away?

### RQ2.3 Missing and unfavorable evidence

- Is the correct state not found, not reported, not studied, not evaluable or
  negative?
- What search record is required before using not found?
- What event triggers re-verification of a claim or program status?

## RQ3 — Which assets and modalities define the competitive field?

- **Owner:** [JHA-144](https://linear.app/jha-res/issue/JHA-144/validate-preliminary-asset-universe)
- **Output:** Validated asset universe, program-status log and evidence-gap list
- **Feeds:** Modality deep dives, catalyst watchlist, scoring rubric, commercial
  analogs and final competitive-whitespace assessment
- **Gates:** G4, G5 and parts of G2

### RQ3.1 Relevance

- Which assets have a defensible mechanism, development or transaction relevance
  to Sjögren B-cell reset?
- Which close functional competitors must be included even when they do not claim
  reset?
- What is the rationale for every inclusion and exclusion?

### RQ3.2 Status

- What are the validated company, asset, alias, target, modality, partner, stage
  and trial identifiers?
- What is the latest public status date?
- Is each program active, discontinued or unclear, and what evidence supports the
  status?

### RQ3.3 Sjögren directness and gaps

- Is development Sjögren-direct, adjacent-autoimmune or preclinical only?
- What critical evidence is missing for classification or comparison?
- Which 12–24 month catalysts could change the universe or a gate result?

## RQ4 — Which patients anchor the opportunity?

- **Owner:** [JHA-146](https://linear.app/jha-res/issue/JHA-146/map-sjogren-patient-segmentation)
- **Output:** Patient-segmentation framework, definitions and caveats
- **Feeds:** Epidemiology, diagnosis/treatment funnel, unmet need, trial design,
  patient–modality decision map and commercial model
- **Gates:** G1, G2, G6

### RQ4.1 Disease context

- What evidence distinguishes primary from secondary Sjögren?
- How should diagnosed and undiagnosed populations be separated?
- Which conclusions are valid for the US primary market and which are EU5
  secondary comparisons?

### RQ4.2 Clinically interpretable segments

- How are glandular-dominant, systemic and severe systemic disease defined?
- How do ESSDAI, ESSPRI, organ involvement and treatment history inform segments
  without being treated as interchangeable?
- How is lymphoma risk analyzed without using it as a general severity proxy?

### RQ4.3 Decision relevance

- Which segments are clinically measurable even if they overlap?
- Which segments offer a plausible mechanism-to-need match for each modality?
- Which biomarkers are hypotheses for enrichment rather than validated selectors?
- Which segment variables can be used in trial and commercial analyses?

## RQ5 — What is the current pathway and unmet-need baseline?

- **Owner:** [JHA-149](https://linear.app/jha-res/issue/JHA-149/map-current-treatment-pathway)
- **Output:** Current-state treatment pathway, therapy-burden table and escalation map
- **Feeds:** Unmet-need assessment, endpoint analysis, pricing/access analogs,
  differentiation claims and final recommendation
- **Gates:** G1, G2, G6

### RQ5.1 Current care

- What supportive and sicca care is used by symptom pattern?
- Where are hydroxychloroquine, corticosteroids, conventional
  immunosuppressants, rituximab and other off-label biologics used?
- How do treatment choices vary by segment and organ involvement?

### RQ5.2 Sequencing and burden

- What are the observed sequencing, escalation, persistence, retreatment and
  monitoring patterns?
- Where do failure, intolerance, discontinuation or burden occur?
- Which observations reflect guidelines and which reflect real-world practice?

### RQ5.3 Comparator implications

- Which therapies have supportive-care versus disease-modifying intent?
- What is the relevant comparator for each proposed patient–modality pairing?
- What differentiation would be clinically meaningful and commercially credible?

## RQ6 — How will the evidence become a recommendation?

- **Owner:** JHA-141 for the framework; downstream synthesis issue to be confirmed
- **Output:** Patient–modality decision map and advance/defer/stop recommendation
- **Feeds:** Final decision deliverable
- **Gates:** G1–G6

### RQ6.1 Integration

- Which patient–modality pairs pass each gate?
- What evidence is direct, indirect, contradictory or missing for each pair?
- Which conclusions are robust across US and EU5 interpretations?

### RQ6.2 Recommendation

- Does any pair meet all minimum requirements for advance?
- If defer, what named evidence or catalyst will trigger re-review?
- If stop, which failed gate is decisive and what rescue evidence would be needed?
- What residual uncertainties must remain visible to the decision maker?

## Completion rule

A downstream workstream is complete only when its output answers the assigned
question, cites the relevant Evidence Ledger rows, identifies unresolved evidence
states and states how the answer changes—or does not change—the final decision.
