## Selection rule

Create one article for each deck session selected for public release after its
anchor is stable. A session is selected only when it supplies all of the
following:

1. one answer-first, decision-relevant thesis;
2. two to four Evidence Ledger anchors that can be disclosed publicly;
3. one material uncertainty, negative result or counterargument;
4. one portfolio, clinical-development or market implication; and
5. additional decision value that remains in the full deck.

A session is not selected when its argument depends on confidential material,
unresolved evidence, a full matrix that cannot be summarized fairly, or a deck
destination that is not available.

## Content boundary

Every article discloses:

- one answer-first thesis;
- two to four evidence anchors;
- one important uncertainty or counterargument;
- one decision implication; and
- a precise statement of what the mapped deck session adds.

The deck retains:

- complete asset and modality matrices;
- full patient segmentation and market model;
- full source and citation apparatus;
- sensitivity analyses and decision-gate scoring;
- detailed methods and appendix; and
- cross-session synthesis and final recommendation.
