# Project charter

## Document control

- **Authorizing issue:** [JHA-141 — Finalize project charter and research questions](https://linear.app/jha-res/issue/JHA-141/finalize-project-charter-and-research-questions)
- **Status:** Approved baseline for downstream work
- **Evidence cutoff:** 2026-10-04
- **Primary geography:** United States
- **Secondary geography:** EU5 (France, Germany, Italy, Spain and the United Kingdom)
- **Permitted evidence:** Public information only

## Decision to be supported

The project will determine whether the available public evidence supports an
**advance, defer or stop** recommendation for continued portfolio diligence on
B-cell reset strategies in Sjögren disease, and identify the patient segment,
modality profile and evidence package required to justify an advance decision.

The recommendation is a portfolio-research decision, not medical advice, an
investment recommendation or a clinical-development approval.

- **Advance:** A credible patient–modality opportunity passes the decision gates
  with decision-grade or explicitly bounded evidence.
- **Defer:** The opportunity remains plausible, but a named evidence gap or
  near-term catalyst prevents a defensible advance or stop decision.
- **Stop:** A critical gate fails, or the evidence needed to rescue the
  opportunity is not realistically obtainable within the decision horizon.

## Decision perspectives

The final synthesis must keep these perspectives distinct before integrating them:

1. Disease biology and clinical unmet need
2. Patient segmentation and treatment pathway
3. Modality mechanism, depth, reach, durability and reconstitution
4. Competitive intensity and program status
5. Development, delivery and safety feasibility
6. Commercial and portfolio relevance
7. Evidence quality, uncertainty and catalyst timing

## Scope

### Geography

- Use the **US as the primary decision market** for epidemiology, treatment
  practice, access, pricing analogs and commercial interpretation.
- Use **EU5 as a secondary comparison set**, reported separately where definitions,
  practice patterns, regulatory context or data availability differ.
- Include global trials and assets when they materially inform biology,
  competition, safety, efficacy or development timing; do not silently treat
  global evidence as US or EU5 market evidence.
- Other geographies may be used only as contextual or indirect evidence and must
  be labeled as such.

### Population

- **Primary Sjögren disease** is the core population for disease burden,
  segmentation, treatment-pathway and opportunity conclusions.
- **Secondary Sjögren** is retained as a separate contextual stratum. It must not
  be pooled with primary Sjögren unless the source reports separable definitions
  and the analytical reason for pooling is documented.
- Diagnosed and undiagnosed populations must be separated.
- Glandular-dominant, systemic and severe systemic disease may overlap; segment
  definitions must state inclusion rules and avoid treating lymphoma risk as a
  generic severity proxy.
- Evidence from adjacent autoimmune diseases is indirect evidence. It may inform
  mechanism, feasibility or analogs, but cannot establish Sjögren efficacy or
  market size.

### Time and sources

- The baseline evidence cutoff is **2026-10-04**. Later evidence requires a logged
  change to the cutoff or a clearly labeled post-cutoff update.
- Only public sources may be used, including registries, peer-reviewed literature,
  guidelines, regulatory materials, conference disclosures and company
  communications.
- Employer-confidential, paywalled licensed data that cannot be independently
  cited, personal data and non-public expert information are out of scope.
- Every material claim or number must be traceable through the Evidence Ledger.

### Included work

- Define an operational B-cell reset taxonomy independent of marketing language.
- Build the claim-level evidence architecture and controlled evidence states.
- Validate the relevant asset and modality universe and current program status.
- Define clinically and commercially meaningful patient segments.
- Map current treatment, sequencing, burden, failure and escalation.
- Integrate downstream disease, market, competitive and portfolio work into a
  patient–modality decision map and recommendation.

### Excluded work

- Primary market research, clinician or patient interviews, and proprietary
  claims-data analysis
- New wet-lab, translational or clinical experiments
- Patient-level treatment recommendations
- Unqualified revenue forecasts or valuation presented as facts
- Exhaustive review of unrelated autoimmune assets without a stated Sjögren
  decision use
- Modification of any future `00_source/` material unless separately authorized

## Inclusion and exclusion criteria

A source, asset, patient segment or comparator is included only when it can answer
a stated research question and its decision relevance is recorded.

Include:

- Sjögren-direct clinical, biomarker, epidemiology, treatment or program evidence
- Adjacent-autoimmune evidence with an explicit transfer rationale and limitations
- Assets targeting B-cell depletion, plasma-cell effects, immune reconstitution,
  tolerance restoration or close functional comparators
- Active, discontinued and unclear programs when status affects the opportunity
- Supportive care, off-label therapy and disease-modifying approaches needed to
  represent the current pathway

Exclude or quarantine:

- Marketing claims without assessable supporting evidence
- Stale registry entries used as sole proof of current program status
- Estimates lacking a usable population, geography, denominator or date
- Pooled primary/secondary Sjögren results that cannot answer the relevant question
- Duplicate claims that add no independent evidence
- Evidence outside the cutoff unless logged as a post-cutoff update

## Decision gates

Each gate receives **pass**, **conditional**, **fail** or **not evaluable**, with
the evidence rows and uncertainty cited.

| Gate | Decision test | Minimum requirement for advance |
|---|---|---|
| G1 Strategic fit | Is there a decision-relevant Sjögren problem a B-cell reset strategy could plausibly address? | Defined patient problem and mechanism-to-need rationale |
| G2 Clinical differentiation | Could the modality improve depth, tissue reach, durability, reconstitution or burden versus current care? | Explicit differentiation hypothesis and measurable endpoints |
| G3 Evidence credibility | Is support traceable, sufficiently direct and robust for the claim made? | No critical conclusion rests only on unverified interpretation |
| G4 Competitive whitespace | Is there a defensible position after active, discontinued and unclear programs are considered? | Validated universe and a stated basis for differentiation |
| G5 Development feasibility | Are safety, delivery, retreatment, manufacturing and trial-design risks plausibly manageable? | No unmitigated fatal feasibility constraint |
| G6 Commercial relevance | Is the addressable segment meaningful under bounded US-primary and EU5-secondary assumptions? | Transparent segment, adoption and access logic without false precision |

A recommendation cannot be **advance** when a critical gate is fail or not
evaluable. A **defer** recommendation must name the evidence or catalyst that
would change the result and the trigger for re-review.

## Evidence language

### Claim types

- **Fact:** Directly supported by the cited source
- **Company interpretation:** An interpretation or claim made by a company
- **Analyst inference:** A conclusion derived from cited evidence
- **Extrapolation:** Evidence transferred from another population, disease,
  geography or modality
- **Unknown:** The available evidence does not support a conclusion

### Evidence states

- **not found:** A defined search did not identify evidence; absence is not proof
  that evidence does not exist.
- **not reported:** The located source does not disclose the item.
- **not studied:** The design did not assess the item.
- **not evaluable:** Evidence exists but cannot support a valid assessment.
- **negative:** The item was assessed and the prespecified or relevant result was
  unfavorable.

These states are not interchangeable and must never be collapsed into zero,
absence or failure without justification.

## Approval and change control

This charter is the approved baseline for downstream work. A change that alters
the decision question, geography, population, cutoff, source restrictions,
deliverables or gate logic must be recorded in the scope and assumption register,
linked to a Linear issue and reflected in affected deliverables. Clarifications
that do not change scope may be committed with the authorizing issue.
